# LT Foods Limited: fundamental and risk analysis

An equity research report on LT Foods Limited (NSE: LTFOODS), the basmati rice company behind the Daawat and Royal brands, covering FY2022-23 and FY2023-24. Written for FIN 4951 Financial and Economic Analysis in my MBA at Middlesex University Dubai.

## Summary

LT Foods grew earnings much faster than revenue over the two years, paid off almost all of its long-term debt, and its share price rose far more than its market risk would predict.

## Fundamentals

Figures are consolidated, in ₹ crore, from the company's financial statements via S&P Capital IQ.

| Ratio | FY2022-23 | FY2023-24 |
|---|---|---|
| Revenue | 6,935.8 | 7,772.4 |
| Revenue growth | 27.8% | 12.1% |
| Net profit | 422.8 | 597.6 |
| Net profit margin | 6.1% | 7.7% |
| EBITDA margin | 10.1% | 12.1% |
| Return on equity | 15.3% | 17.7% |
| Return on capital employed | 18.2% | 22.4% |
| Asset turnover | 1.31x | 1.29x |
| Inventory turnover | 4.01x | 4.11x |
| Current ratio | 2.00x | 2.10x |
| Quick ratio | 0.55x | 0.62x |
| Debt to equity (long-term debt) | 0.10x | 0.003x |
| Earnings per share | ₹11.60 | ₹17.09 |

What changed:

- **Earnings:** EPS rose 47.3% on 12.1% revenue growth. Freight costs fell after the pandemic, and India lifted its minimum export price on basmati rice in September 2023.
- **Debt:** long-term debt fell from ₹260.9 crore to ₹10.5 crore.
- **Liquidity:** the quick ratio is low because basmati rice is aged in inventory before sale, which is normal for the sector.

## Risk and return

`lt-foods-risk-analysis.xlsx` holds 105 weekly returns from April 2022 to March 2024 for LT Foods, the Nifty 50 and the India 10-year government bond, with the formulas for each statistic below.

| Measure | LT Foods | Nifty 50 |
|---|---|---|
| Average weekly return | 1.06% | 0.25% |
| Weekly standard deviation | 5.76% | 1.68% |
| Correlation with Nifty 50 | 0.24 | |
| Beta (slope of LT Foods on Nifty 50) | 0.83 | |

With an average risk-free rate of about 7.25% a year, CAPM gives an expected return of about 11.8% a year. The stock's average weekly return works out to roughly 55% a year over the period. A beta below one and a correlation of 0.24 say most of that move was specific to the company: the export rule change, the debt paydown and the earnings growth.
